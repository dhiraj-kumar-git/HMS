Using the Patient Portal

Register yourself and book a doctor's appointment online
For students, staff & families

1

Opening the portal

No login needed for this page — anyone can open it from a phone or computer.

  1. 1Open the portal address given by the Medical Centre (ask reception if you don't have it).
  2. 2The Medical Center Information Board on the left has OPD timings, the doctor roster, weekly schedules, and emergency numbers — tap between the tabs.
  3. 3The Book an Appointment card on the right is where you'll spend most of your time.
Everything a patient needs, without logging in
💡Already have staff credentials? Use Clinic Staff Login at the bottom of the card instead — this page is for patients.
2

Registering for the first time

Only needed once. If you already have an Institute ID or PSRN No. registered with the Medical Centre, skip to Step 3.

  1. 1From the portal, choose the self-registration option for your category (student or staff).
  2. 2Fill in your BITS Institute ID, Full Name, BITS Email ID, Patient Type, date of birth, gender, contact number, and hostel/address.
  3. 3Click Complete Registration. You're taken back to the portal automatically.
A one-time form — use your real BITS email, it's how you'll receive OTPs
3

Booking an appointment

Click Book an Appointment on the portal home page.

  1. 1Type your Institute ID / PSRN No. and click Verify Patient.
  2. 2Check your registered email for a 4-digit code, and enter it to verify it's really you.
  3. 3Pick a doctor from Doctors Available Today and click Book Now, or use Schedule for a Later Date if your doctor isn't on duty right now.
  4. 4Confirm the time slot. Your appointment is booked.
Start here — your ID unlocks the rest of the booking flow
⚠After booking online, visit the Reception Desk to confirm your arrival and collect your prescription slip printout — booking online alone doesn't check you in.

Getting help

Not receiving your OTP email, or a screen looks different from this guide? Contact the Reception Desk using the numbers on the portal's information board — don't keep retrying. This guide was captured directly from the system on 8 September 2026.
